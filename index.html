Built around real driving moments

Less uncertainty. More road ahead.

Not another list of charging pins. PlugPulse is designed to explain whether a charger is usable — and what to do when plans change.

01 — Availability

Is it actually free?

Check the status of each connector, not just whether a charging location appears on the map.

02 — Fault awareness

Will it actually work?

Separate reported faults, stale status, and successful charging signals so drivers understand reliability.

03 — Better routes

What if plans change?

Compare estimated waiting time and locate a backup before wasting time and battery range.
